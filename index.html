Delete Account

Last updated: October 16, 2026 (draft for owner review)

You can delete your AFGHANMARKET account from inside the Android app.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the AFGHANMARKET app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account (it appears only when you are signed in).
  4. Read the warning and confirm.

If deletion fails, you stay signed in and can try again. After a successful deletion you are signed out.

What is deleted

Deleting your account is intended to remove your login, your profile, your listings, notifications, reviews, reports, blocked-user entries and the files you uploaded. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What may remain

Our cleanup is not yet confirmed to cover every type of data, for example messages, orders, promotion orders and some uploaded images stored in subfolders. Some of this may remain after your account is deleted. We will update this page once it is confirmed.
